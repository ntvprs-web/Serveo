About SERVEO

A crowdsourced, location-based platform designed to centralize and map nearby public-service and community welfare initiatives in real time — bridging the gap between willing citizens and the grassroots initiatives that need participation.

🤝

Our Mission

Every day, thousands of public welfare initiatives take place across communities, yet most suffer from severely diminished turnout due to fragmented communication channels.

SERVEO exists to solve this visibility crisis. We believe that the gap between "wanting to help" and "actually helping" is primarily a discovery problem — and we're building the technology to close it.

By combining interactive map discovery, real-time geolocation, verified organizer portals, and urgency indicators, SERVEO transforms how communities connect with the initiatives that need them most.

🗓️ Development Roadmap

A phased approach from MVP to scaled municipal deployment.

Phase 0 — Complete

Architecture Setup & Foundation

Repository setup, design system, component library, asset pipelines, and PWA scaffolding.

Phase 1 — In Progress

Core Interface & Map Engine

Leaflet.js deployment with OpenStreetMap, browser geolocation "Near Me" centering, and 18 seeded regional events.

Phase 2 — Upcoming

Backend, Database & Event Submissions

Firebase/Supabase integration, authenticated "Post an Event" workflow, dynamic database queries, and live map-marker population.

Phase 3 — Planned

Event Discovery, UX & Engagement

Haversine distance sorting, dynamic category filters, urgency tags, event detail popups, native directions, and social sharing.

Phase 4 — Future

PWA Transformation & Scalability

Service worker offline persistence, FCM push notifications, pilot launch in target city, and performance optimization.
