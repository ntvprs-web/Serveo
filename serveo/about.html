About SERVEO

A crowdsourced, location-based platform designed to centralize and map nearby public-service and community welfare initiatives in real time — bridging the gap between willing citizens and the grassroots initiatives that need participation.

🤝

Our Mission

Every day, thousands of public welfare initiatives take place across communities, yet most suffer from severely diminished turnout due to fragmented communication channels.

SERVEO exists to solve this visibility crisis. We believe that the gap between "wanting to help" and "actually helping" is primarily a discovery problem — and we're building the technology to close it.

By combining interactive map discovery, real-time geolocation, verified organizer portals, and urgency indicators, SERVEO transforms how communities connect with the initiatives that need them most.

🗓️ Development Milestones

Tracked progress from concept to a fully operational civic welfare system.

Phase 1 — Complete

Interactive Map & Proximity Engine

Mapbox GL v3 terrain integration, custom teardrop pin generator, Haversine proximity calculations, and category filtering.

Phase 2 — Complete

Cloud Database & Real-Time Sync

Firebase Firestore database schema, real-time snapshot sync, live event submission workflow, and organizer approval queue.

Phase 3 — Complete

0–5km Alerting, Navigation & Localization

Automated 0–5 km geofenced proximity alerts, direct Google Maps turn-by-turn routing, and 12-language Indian localization.

PWA Offline Resilience & Impact Telemetry

Service Worker asset caching for emergency zones, Chart.js impact analytics, and municipal scalability rollout.
