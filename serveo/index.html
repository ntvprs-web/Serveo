Purpose-Built Features

Every feature is designed to solve a specific piece of the visibility crisis.

🗺️

Real-Time Map Discovery

Interactive map with categorized pins for blood drives, medical camps, food hubs, and more. Marker clustering keeps the view clean.

📍

Geolocation "Near Me"

Uses your browser's GPS to instantly find events around your current location, sorted by distance using the Haversine formula.

✅

Verified Organizers

Trust badges for certified NGOs, hospitals, and government bodies. Prevents scam postings and builds donor confidence.

🔴

Urgency Indicators

Real-time status tags — "Happening Now", "Ending Soon", "Critical Shortage" — so you can act on what matters most.

🧭

Instant Directions

One-tap navigation to any event via Google Maps, Apple Maps, or your default navigation app. Zero SDK overhead.

📊

Impact Analytics

Public dashboard showing total events, volunteer hours, blood units collected, and geographic service coverage.
